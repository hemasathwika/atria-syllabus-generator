Outcome-Based Education (OBE)

Standard 5-CO structure with cognitive (CL1–CL6) and psychomotor (PL1–PL3) levels mapped to CIE/SEE examinations.

⏱️
Student Learning Time (SLT)

Formulated as Lecture (L) + Tutorial (T) + Practical (P) + Self-Study (S) = Total Hours, validated against AICTE credit limits.

⚖️
Equal Assessment Weightage

Balanced 50 Marks Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and 50 Marks Semester End Examination (SEE) for 100 total marks.
